About Snow

Snow forms when atmospheric water vapor freezes into ice crystals. These crystals then grow and fall to the ground as snowflakes.

No two snowflakes are exactly alike, each with a unique pattern and structure.

Snow Facts

Record Snowfall

The highest recorded seasonal snowfall in the US was 1,140 inches (95 feet) at Mount Baker, Washington in 1998-99.

Snow Colors

Snow can appear in different colors, including pink, orange, green, and even black, due to algae or mineral deposits.

Sound Absorption

Fresh snow absorbs sound, making the world seem quieter after a snowfall.
